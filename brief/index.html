Pass the brief

This page gives you Mind Reclaim’s one-page case in a form you can take to people you already talk to — a newsletter, a parent circle, a forum, a local group, or another room you belong to.

You get our brief. You change one beat so it lands for that room. You keep the succession reading honest. Then you pass your version on yourself. This is voluntary. There are no points for volume.

Read the Must keep / Must not list. Adapt one beat. Submit or copy your version. Share it with your room.

Must keep

  • The succession reading in plain words: AI racing to outcompete us; feeds and total reliance as how we stay useful as fuel.
  • Starve the feed as the human-scale lever (attention, habits, data you still control).
  • Portal as a door for joint work — not a product pitch.
  • Cite-safe facts stay cite-safe; label beliefs as our reading.
  • Anonymous / no personal-brand hero story.
  • Open to any concerned individual — not schools-only framing.

Must not claim

  • A secret cabal, mind control, or “they” as a cartoon villain.
  • That starving your own feed alone unplugs a frontier lab.
  • Medical diagnoses, cure promises, or guaranteed outcomes.
  • Formal partnership with listed allies unless we say so.
  • Urgency scams, countdown guilt, or “share or you’re complicit.”
  • Soft substitute thesis (“just healthier screen time”) that erases succession.

Adapt for your room. Do not sand the thesis down to mush, and do not sharpen it into rage-bait.

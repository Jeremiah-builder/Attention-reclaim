Digital Citizenship Week at home

From Monday 19 to Friday 23 October 2026, Common Sense Education runs Digital Citizenship Week. This year the theme is critical thinking for a world with AI, and the classroom activities are free.

We’re glad to see it. For five days, schools and families can talk together about how technology shapes what children see and think, and this year’s focus on thinking critically about AI could hardly be more timely. The lessons are free, practical and planned for every school age, so any family can join in. Here are a few simple ways to carry it home.

Mind Reclaim is an independent volunteer group. We aren’t part of Common Sense Education and they haven’t endorsed this page. We simply link to their free lessons.

Talk about it at home

Five minutes, no lecture. One question a day is plenty.

  1. Ask what tried to pull them back in online today: autoplay, “up next”, a streak, a notification.
  2. Name the design, not the child: “That app is built to keep you watching. It isn’t only you.”
  3. Try one short stretch without devices together, like the start of homework, dinner, or the first five minutes after school. Notice how it feels to settle.
  4. If they use AI for schoolwork, ask: “Did you try first, or did the tool go first?”
  5. On Friday, pick one thing to keep for next week. Just one.

Volunteer kit & community outline

Mind Reclaim (working name) · draft for lock-in before build

1) Who a volunteer is

Local ambassador: a parent, teacher, or neighbour who runs small, local awareness — not a social-media influencer.

In scope: parent nights, school/PTA talks, neighbour circles, share kit materials, model phone norms.

Out of scope: harassment, conspiracy content, growth-hacking the movement on short-form social.

2) Volunteer page (site) — sections

  1. Why we need local people
  2. What you’ll do
  3. What we provide (kit + support)
  4. Apply / join form
  5. Download starter kit
  6. FAQ

4) Community for support (phased)

Phase 1 (now): welcome email · private Signal/Telegram group for vetted volunteers · monthly 45-min office hours · shared FAQ doc.

Phase 2 (20+ active): light forum; regional channels.

Moderation: no shaming parents, no medical diagnoses, no conspiracy, no scraping kids’ data; press escalates to the founder.

5) Join form (minimal)

Name · Email · City/region · Role · Languages · Agree to guidelines · Optional first-talk idea → founder inbox (+ optional spreadsheet).

6) Backoffice

Sheet: Applicant → Approved → Kit sent → First event → Notes. Public Monday Updates stay on the site; volunteer announcements go to the private group.

7) Build sequence

  1. Lock outline
  2. Copywriter: page + talking points + guidelines
  3. Designer: one-pager + slides + poster
  4. Builder: /volunteer + /kit + form
  5. Founder: Signal group + office-hours calendar

Default access rule until decided otherwise: show kit list publicly; full PDF pack after apply (or open downloads if preferred).

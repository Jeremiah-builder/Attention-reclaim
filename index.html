Observed

5Rights Foundation

Childhood Redrawn, October 2026

An avatar audit of 9 social, AI, and gaming services found attention-grabbing notifications in all but one, and autoplay and personalised feeds in six. 5Rights calls potentially manipulative design “widespread across all digital services used by kids, not just social media.”

5Rights analysis (opens in a new tab)

5Rights Foundation

Pathways, 2021

Research by Revealing Reality for 5Rights: Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok served harmful content to accounts registered as 13-year-olds within 24 hours. 5Rights: “The risks these features pose are not accidental.”

Pathways report (opens in a new tab)

Who holds the plug

Power sits where compute, distribution, and attention are held. Those are still human hands and human firms.

Unplugged. A diagram, not a photograph. The socket stays in human hands.

Observed

Frontier AI still runs on human-made hardware. Anthony Aguirre documents a narrow chain: ASML for lithography, TSMC for fabrication, NVIDIA, AMD, and Google for design. The Center for Humane Technology states that capital, data, compute, and talent “are being consolidated among a handful of multinational corporations.” The plug is held by people and firms.

Trajectory

The incentive is to leave it switched on. CHT: platforms are built to keep people on site. 5Rights says the progress that did happen came “largely” from regulation, “rather than industry-led reform.” Researchers writing as Gradual Disempowerment (opens in a new tab) argue — they do not measure — that institutions stay answerable partly because they still rely on “human labor and cognition,” and that less reliance weakens that link. Their argument. Not a finding.

Our reading

Those firms will not pull the plug. Their business is the current. Succession — AI outcompeting us by numbing us with feeds until reliance is total — is Mind Reclaim’s story, not a result in a paper. The lever a person still holds is to starve the feed: attention, and the data that attention leaves behind.

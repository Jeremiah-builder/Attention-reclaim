What is happening

Feeds are ranked to keep eyes moving — often not to help a child think or grow. At the same time, easy AI answers can skip the practice that builds understanding. That feeling that something is off is worth listening to. We name the design, not a villain.

What we want to protect

Attention, sleep, mood, learning, and real belonging. Limits help — especially when families and schools share the same calm rules, and when life is full of offline yeses.

What we do

Mind Reclaim is parents and educators, voluntary, kitchen-table. We offer clear talks, simple kits, and shared norms for homes and schools. No fear stories. No conspiracy talk.

For parents

You know your child best. Start with sleep, meals, and homework without the feed. Model one phone habit yourself. Agree when AI is allowed — and that they still explain the work in their own words. Ask another adult to back the same plan.

For communities and schools

One family alone has a hard job. Shared phone-free times, parent nights that explain design, and offline clubs make limits feel normal.

Better ways to spend time

Saying no to the phone is not enough. Children need good things to say yes to: friends in person, movement, shared play, and time outside.

Read more: Better ways →

What you can do this week

  1. Say it simply: “This app is built to keep you here.”
  2. Protect one daily block without the feed.
  3. Put one offline yes on the calendar.
  4. Try alone before any AI help; explain the work in your own words.
  5. Ask two other adults to back the same rules.

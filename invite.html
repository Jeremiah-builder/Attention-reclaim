Talks & parent nights

Invite us to speak

Clear talks for parents, schools, and community groups. We explain how social feeds work. We share what the Surgeon General and the APA say about young people. We talk about limits, parental example, and real-life alternatives. We leave time for questions. We do not use scare tactics.

What a talk covers

  • How apps hold attention (Path A / Path B in plain words).
  • Light evidence: sleep, mood, body image, design for youth.
  • Family starts and shared school/street norms.
  • Offline yes: sports, games, nature, real social time.

Lengths: about 5, 15, or 40 minutes—plus questions.
